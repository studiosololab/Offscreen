# Offscreen
Fast, private offline video player. No ads, no internet, no tracking.

Full description:
Offscreen is a clean, fast video player for the videos already on your phone. It has no ads, needs no internet connection and collects nothing about you.

Easy to use

• Swipe to seek, swipe the left side for brightness and the right side for volume

• Double-tap to skip, hold to play at 2x, pinch to fill the screen

• Lock the controls so touches can't interrupt a video

• Resume where you left off, with progress bars and a Continue watching row


Organise your videos

• Folders, a library, search, list and grid views

• Playlists, favourites and a watch history

• Unwatched and watched filters, sorting, and NEW tags for fresh videos

• Back up your playlists and settings to a file and restore them anytime

• Recycle bin, so deleted videos can be restored
Private and protected

• Hide videos in a Private folder protected by a PIN or fingerprint. Hidden videos are stored inside the app, so restore them to your gallery before uninstalling.


Watch your way
• Subtitles from files or built into the video, with adjustable size, colour, position and delay

• Playback speed from 0.25x to 3x

• Equalizer, bass boost and volume boost up to 200%

• Pop-up player, picture-in-picture and background audio

• Sleep timer, A-B repeat, bookmarks, screenshots and a night filter

• Light, dark and pure black themes with your choice of accent colour


Works with other apps

Open a video from your file manager or any other app and play it in Offscreen.

Offscreen plays most common video formats. A few rare audio formats, such as DTS, may not be supported on every phone, and the app will tell you when that happens.


Questions or ideas? Use Send feedback in Settings.
